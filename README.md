# Couch Clicker Atomic 2

The fancy keyboard for your couch.

Couch Clicker Atomic 2 is a Bluetooth, mechanical key, home theater remote control designed for use with [Amity](https://github.com/retsyx/amity) - home theater control over HDMI-CEC that works! (really)

## Using Couch Clicker Atomic 2

Couch Clicker Atomic 2 works with Amity like any other compatible remote. To read more about Amity and its operation, visit the [Amity page](https://github.com/retsyx/amity).

To clear Couch Clicker Atomic 2's Bluetooth pairing and to re-pair, press and hold the `Power` and `Volume Up` buttons for 6 seconds.

To reset Couch Clicker Atomic 2, press and hold the `Power` and `Mute` buttons for 6 seconds.

### ZMK

Download the ZMK firmware image from TBD.

When the remote is fully assembled in the case, to update the firmware, the remote must be reset to the bootloader. Press and hold the `Power`, `Select` and `Back` buttons for 10 seconds to reset to the bootloader.
